# 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

## Hook (0:00 - 0:30)
- "Universities waste over 30% of their operational energy heating, cooling, and lighting empty lecture halls. Traditional smart buildings rely on invasive cameras or expensive IoT sensors. Today, we present **Campus Carbon Twin**."

## Live Edge Detection (0:30 - 1:15)
- Navigate to **Live Edge Camera** tab.
- Show laptop webcam detecting the presenter (`personCount: 1`).
- Step out of frame or cover camera (`personCount: 0`).
- Show the network panel: "Notice zero images leave the machine — only an anonymous, zero-PII occupancy vector."

## Digital Twin Waste Detection (1:15 - 2:00)
- Switch to **Campus Overview**.
- Room `SCI-204` turns orange (`WASTEFUL_OCCUPANCY`): HVAC is drawing 3.5 kW with 0 occupants for over 10 minutes.
- Click into `SCI-204` to inspect the live equipment breakdown and live carbon emissions rate.

## Action Proposal & LLM Explanation (2:00 - 2:40)
- View the **Action Timeline**.
- Highlight the proposed action: "Eco-Mode HVAC Setback".
- Show the AI explanation generated by Gemini detailing why the action is safe and how much $CO_2$ and dollars it will save.
- Click **Approve & Execute**. The room status returns to `OPTIMAL`.

## What-If Scenario Simulator & Wrap Up (2:40 - 3:00)
- Show the **Scenario Simulator**: Toggle campus-wide automated night setback and see immediate monthly savings of 14.2 tons of $CO_2e$.
- "Campus Carbon Twin delivers immediate carbon mitigation, respects student privacy, and costs 90% less than legacy BMS."
